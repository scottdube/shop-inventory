Template 9 is a BIN-CODE label: it hard-truncates location.name at 22 characters and picks a font size from the character count, maxing out at 6mm for names of 10 or fewer. That is right for `L2-D2` and wrong for every kit box in the shop -- `Kit - Glarks 80pc 2.54mm Double Row Female Socket` renders as `Kit - Glarks 80pc 2.5...`, which drops double row, female and socket, i.e. everything that tells you what is in the box. The other three kit boxes have the same problem waiting for them: `Kit - EMGTMS 24-Value Ceramic` (29 chars), `Kit - SMD Rectifier Bridge 8-value` (34), `Kit - 10-Value Electrolytic 4x7` (31). So the name WRAPS here, at a fixed small size, over three lines. Use `{% comment %}`, never `{# #}` -- see LABELLING.md.
