## How to work it

Sorted by **McMaster number**, because that is the key physically present in
the drawer. Brady labelling is incomplete; where a drawer has no Brady label,
Scott kept the McMaster bag tag inside it in most cases. The tag number is
unique to one row here — a Brady label reading "M4 button head" is not.

Write the drawer in the left column. If a drawer has neither a label nor a
tag, leave it and note the drawer separately — do not guess from appearance;
M4x10 and M4x12 button heads are indistinguishable in a bin.
