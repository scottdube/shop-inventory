### Why the GMA1347 control board is redundant, and why that is not obvious

The GMA1347 **audio panel is still correct for NXi** — commercial NXi panel sets
still ship one, and FSD still sells the FSD GMA1347. What changed is *what
drives it*. In the original architecture the audio panel had its own controller
board and its own Arduino. In NXi, the shield absorbs that: `U4 MEGA 2560 PRO —
GMA1347`.

So the **panel** carries over and the **control board** does not. That
distinction is the whole answer, and it is easy to get backwards.

Build #2 settles it by demonstration rather than inference — the shield drives
the audio panel over two ribbons, with no separate control board present.

### The GMA1347 faceplate

The "GMA-1347 Blank (2) PCB Set" was the design for control board **plus**
faceplate. Only the control board has surfaced as a physical PCB. Either the
faceplate Gerber was never sent to JLCPCB, or it was and the boards are in the
same stack. **The faceplate is still wanted for an NXi build**, and if it was
never fabbed the files are already paid for — it is a JLCPCB order away, not a
repurchase.

### What to 3D print for a PANEL build — settled from the instructions

**FSD ships two mutually exclusive mounting systems and only one is Scott's.**
`Desktop Mount Parts` is a standalone enclosure — legs, rear cases, cover
plates. `Panel Mount Parts` is the one that goes into the Cessna panel.
**Printing from the wrong folder is the easy mistake here**, and the folder
names do not warn you: both contain a `Backplate PCB Standoffs.stl` and a
`Bottom Center PCB Holder.stl` with identical names and different geometry.

**Everything behind the panel, one full set per unit — this IS the whole
`Panel Mount Parts` folder, all 10 files:**

| STL | Role | Step |
|---|---|---|
| `Top LCD Bracket` | clamp the LCD to the faceplate rear | 3 |
| `Left LCD Bracket` | same | 3 |
| `Right LCD Bracket` | same | 3 |
| `Bottom Center PCB Holder` | glued to the bottom faceplate on locator pins | 4 |
| `Panel Mount Backplate` | carries the LCD driver boards | 4 |
| `Left PCB Holders Sprue` | traps the PCBs onto the faceplate | 6 |
| `Right PCB Holders Sprue` | same | 6 |
| `Backplate PCB Standoffs` | LCD controller boards to the backplate | 11 |
| `Left panel mount bracket` | the unit into the sim panel | 12 |
| `Right panel mount bracket` | same | 12 |

Front of panel, for completeness: faceplate (1-part or 4-part), buttons, soft
keys, `Knobs 1of2` + `2of2`, and `SD Card dummy (2x)`.

**The two "Sprue" parts print as a runner** and are cut apart — that is why the
tool list calls for cutting tools. Not a print failure.

**The GMA1347 needs NO printed parts in a panel build.** The archive's
`Modified parts for gma1347/` holds only a left and right *rear case*, and rear
cases exist only in the desktop enclosure. Same reasoning retires Little
Helper's rear case video-board mod for this build.
